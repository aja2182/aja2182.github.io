About me

I'm a sociologist and postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Population Health at NYU Grossman School of Medicine. I study the often-hidden obstacles that stand between people and the behavioral healthcare that is, at least nominally, available to them. My current work focuses on the experience and outcomes of young adults in substance use treatment. I am developing an independent research program that focuses on evaluating and improving treatment practices and outcomes among young people with co-occurring ADHD and substance use disorder.

I combine ethnography with large-scale administrative data. My work has appeared in Social Science & Medicine, Ethnography, Stigma and Health, and leading journals in harm reduction and addiction medicine. I received my Ph.D. in Sociology from Columbia in 2023 and my B.A. from Bard College.

This work is personal for me. I also founded Recovery Sciences, a consultancy that brings this research to recovery residences and treatment programs.

I was born and raised in Brooklyn. Reach out if you'd like to collaborate or swap working papers.
