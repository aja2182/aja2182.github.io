About me

I'm a sociologist and postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Population Health at NYU Grossman School of Medicine. I study the obstacles that stand between people and addiction and mental health care that is, at least nominally, available to them. I am developing an independent research program on ADHD care for young adults in outpatient substance use treatment: who gets identified and treated, whether ADHD medication helps people stay in treatment, and how programs can better serve this population.

I combine ethnography with large-scale administrative data. My work has appeared in Social Science & Medicine, Ethnography, Stigma and Health, and leading journals in harm reduction and addiction medicine. I received my Ph.D. in Sociology from Columbia in 2023 and my B.A. from Bard College.

I also founded Recovery Sciences, a consultancy that helps recovery residences and treatment programs put research into practice.

I was born and raised in Brooklyn. Reach out if you'd like to collaborate or swap working papers.
